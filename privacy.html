Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mathmerize Publisher (the "App") handles information. The App is operated by Sebastián Nieto ("we", "us"), creator of the Mathmerize channels. It is a private tool that publishes Mathmerize's own videos to Mathmerize's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The App is not offered to the public, and only the account owner signs in to it.

Information the App accesses

When the account owner connects an account through the platform's official sign-in (OAuth), the App receives:

  • Access tokens issued by Google (YouTube), TikTok and Meta (Instagram). They allow the App to publish on the connected account.
  • Basic account information: YouTube channel name and ID; TikTok username, display name, avatar and the posting options returned by TikTok (privacy levels, interaction settings and maximum video length); Instagram username and account ID.
  • Content provided by the owner: the video file, title, description, hashtags and posting settings entered for each upload.
  • Publishing results: post IDs, links and error messages returned by each platform.

The App does not access viewers' data, private messages, contacts, analytics of other users, or any account other than the ones the owner connects.

How the information is used

Information is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's connected accounts, to show the owner which account is connected, and to report whether each publication succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Tokens and publishing history are stored locally on the owner's computer, in files that are not shared or uploaded anywhere else. Video files are kept temporarily while they are being published and are deleted afterwards. Information is sent only to the platform being published to (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta), over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties. Information is only exchanged with the platform APIs listed above, as needed to publish content.

Third-party services

The App uses the following services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply:

  • YouTube API Services. By using the App's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data according to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / Instagram (Instagram Graph API): Instagram Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform:

  • Google: Google Account permissions.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites.

Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless. To request deletion of any stored information, email mathmerizechannel@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by the adult owner of the Mathmerize accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
